Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GTA Clip Farmer ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. GTA Clip Farmer is a desktop app that runs on your own Windows computer. It is operated by Trainer Gamer.

1. The short version

  • The app runs only on your computer. We do not run any servers that receive your data.
  • We do not collect, sell, rent or share your personal information.
  • Your sign-in tokens and videos stay on your computer, except for the clips you choose to upload to your own accounts.
  • You can disconnect the app from any of your accounts at any time.

2. Information the app uses

To work, the app uses the following information. All of it is stored only on your computer.

  • Gameplay recordings: your Xbox game clips (downloaded through the OpenXBL service using an API key you provide) or videos in a folder you choose.
  • Edited clips: short videos the app creates from your recordings.
  • Sign-in tokens: access tokens that let the app act on your behalf with Google/YouTube, TikTok, Instagram (Meta) and Microsoft Outlook.com. These are stored in the app's "secrets" folder on your computer.
  • Settings and history: your settings file, and a local record of which clips were made and where they were posted.
  • Your email address: used only to send alert emails to you from your own Outlook.com / Hotmail account.

3. How the app uses Google and YouTube data

If you connect YouTube, the app asks for the youtube.upload permission. It uses this permission only to upload clips you created to your own YouTube channel, along with a title, description and tags. The app does not read, download, change or delete your other YouTube videos, comments, subscribers or account information.

GTA Clip Farmer uses YouTube API Services. By using the YouTube features of the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

GTA Clip Farmer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at Google's security settings page.

4. How the app uses TikTok data

If you connect TikTok, the app asks for the user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish permissions. It uses them only to check which privacy options your account allows and to post clips you created to your own TikTok account. You can remove the app's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (the exact menu names may vary by app version).

5. How the app uses Instagram (Meta) data

If you connect Instagram, the app uses the access token you provide only to publish clips you created as Reels on your own Instagram Professional account. You can remove access in your Facebook settings under Business integrations, or by deleting the token from the app.

6. How the app uses Microsoft data

If you turn on email alerts, the app asks for the Mail.Send permission for your Outlook.com / Hotmail account. It uses this permission only to send alert emails to the address you set in the app. It does not read your inbox, contacts or other mail. You can remove access at Microsoft's app permissions page.

7. Xbox data

The app uses the OpenXBL API key you provide to list and download your own Xbox game clips. OpenXBL is a third-party service with its own privacy policy. The app does not access your Xbox friends, messages or purchases.

8. Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent or trade your information with anyone. The only data that leaves your computer is:

  • the clips, titles and captions the app uploads to your own accounts on the platforms you connect; and
  • the requests the app makes to those platforms' official APIs to do this.

Once a clip is posted, the platform's own privacy policy applies to it.

9. How long information is kept and how to delete it

Everything stays on your computer until you delete it. To delete all app data:

  • delete the app folder (including the "data" and "secrets" folders); and
  • remove the app's access using the links in sections 3 to 6.

Clips already posted to your accounts can be deleted directly on each platform.

10. Security

Sign-in tokens are stored only on your computer. Keep your computer and the app's "secrets" folder private. The app connects to platforms only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

11. Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age required by the platforms it connects to. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
