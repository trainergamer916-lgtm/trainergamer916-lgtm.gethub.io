GTA Clip Farmer

GTA Clip Farmer is a personal desktop app for Windows. It finds highlight moments in the user's own Grand Theft Auto VI gameplay recordings from Xbox, turns them into short clips, and posts them to the user's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

What the app does

  • Downloads the user's own Xbox game captures (through the OpenXBL service) or reads recordings from a folder on the user's PC.
  • Finds highlight moments and edits them into short vertical and widescreen clips on the user's PC.
  • Uploads those clips to the user's own YouTube channel, TikTok account and Instagram account, using each platform's official API.
  • Sends the user email alerts from their own Outlook.com / Hotmail account.

Your data stays on your PC

The app runs only on the user's own computer. It has no servers of its own and sends no data to its developer. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
